How it works
From farm gate to compliance report
Every medicine that leaves a practice, every farm visit, every scheduled drug — tracked, reconciled, and ready for inspection.
1
Farm places monthly order
Farms submit their medicine order through their portal. The practice reviews, approves, and dispatches — all logged automatically.
2
Vet visits, medicines dispensed
Herd visit records, treatments administered, and withdrawal periods are captured in the field or back at the practice. Batch numbers tracked throughout.
3
Withdrawal alerts fire automatically
When an animal or batch is in a withdrawal period, the farm and vet are both alerted. Milk and meat safety protected without manual tracking.
4
Compliance reports, one click
Monthly scheduled drug register, ready for DALRRD inspection. Full audit trail. No paperwork.
